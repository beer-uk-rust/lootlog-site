Star Trek: Outposts Unknown — first log: what I've learned so far

One abandoned save, one fresh start, and a lot of "why won't this build?" Here's what I wish I'd known on day one.

27 October 2026, updated 5 November · Everything below was checked in my own game unless it says otherwise.

Update: Day 70. Level 7 done, and the chip wall is beaten. Ops Level 7 is finished. In the end the Duotronic Chips didn't come from a lucky resupply. They came from a High Tech Factory. Next stop is Level 8, which needs 750 Outpost Proficiency. Here's what I picked up on the way:
  • The chip recipe. The High Tech Factory's Craft Duotronic Chips line takes two kinds of material, 2 of each, and makes 2 chips. It gets a boost from the Computer. Once it's going it fills up fast: when I checked, it was showing Max Inventory with chips waiting to be collected. So keep some utility crew free to carry them away.
  • Your ship's crew can go hungry without you noticing. I got "Mess hall has run out of food". On the ship, the Mess Hall has a Food Proteins Capacity and a Quality Meal Capacity, and mine were both set to 0, so no food was ever sent up. Press + on both and the game sends food up by itself (you can see it under Storage → Inbound).
  • The + on Utility Crew doesn't add anyone. My utility crew had dropped to 2 on days and 2 on nights. Pressing + just moved someone from the other shift. To get more haulers, take crew out of a building that isn't doing anything (see Crew).
  • Don't staff the Advanced Laboratory until it has something to work with. Mine had 3 crew sitting there "Lacking Resources". More on that in the lab section.
  • Check the game speed before you walk away. I'd left mine on fast-forward.
Next up: Level 8, and finding Exploration Points for Advanced Tech Points.
Update: Day 53. The Level 7 wall: Duotronic Chips. I reached the 500 Outpost Proficiency for Ops Level 7 and started the upgrade, and it stopped dead at 0 of 20. It needs 18 Polyalloy and 2 Duotronic Chips, and I had no chips on the planet or the ship. Here's what I found:
  • Only two ways to get chips. Hover the chip icon on the upgrade and it lists the sources: a High Tech Factory or Resupply.
  • The High Tech Factory isn't available yet. It isn't unlocked at Level 7 or Level 8, and Level 7 is the upgrade that needs the chips. Chicken and egg.
  • So it's down to Resupply, and that's luck. Each resupply offers a random selection of crew and resources, and you pick from what's offered. You can only take chips when they turn up, so keep the resupplies coming.
  • Where to find Resupply: the first icon on the bottom-centre bar, the square one just left of the build tabs. It shows what's on its way and a countdown. When it arrives, press Energize.
  • Nothing to switch off. The Operations Center has no on/off switch and no assigned staff. Only utility crew are used, to carry materials in. So get the 18 Polyalloy delivered now, and the build can start the moment the chips land.
  • Look for crew marked Idle. While I was waiting, several crew showed as Idle, still assigned to buildings with nothing to do, with 76 jobs in the queue. Work out why the building has stopped first (no inputs, or full storage), then move spare hands to utility crew as in the Day 50 update.
Solved: the chips came from a High Tech Factory in the end. See the Day 70 update.
Update, 1 November: turn off what you don't need. This one has been a big bottleneck for me. When a facility's storage fills up, it stops producing, and that holds up everything downstream of it.
  • Check for facilities with full storage. If a building is maxed out on something you don't need right now, it's just sitting there stuck.
  • Before an upgrade, look at what it actually needs. Any resource the upgrade (or your next build) doesn't use, turn its production off.
  • Turn it back on when it's needed. Switch production back on as soon as an upgrade, build or requisition calls for that resource.
That keeps storage free for the things that matter, and keeps your crew and haulers working on what you actually need.
Update: Day 50. The outpost on Daegus (X'Lehari system) is now at Ops Level 6 with 37 crew, double the 18 I had at Day 16, and morale is up from about 59 to 76. Three things I've learned since:
  • Watch your utility crew as you grow. With 37 crew I still only had 4 utility crew, all on the day shift and none at night, with 67 jobs queued. Haulers don't scale up by themselves. This is exactly what sank my first save, so check Crew Overview → Tasks → Utility Crew regularly.
  • Tier 3 research needs Advanced Tech Points. Every Tier 3 item costs ordinary Tech Data plus Advanced Tech Points (the orange icon). These come from the Advanced Laboratory (unlocked at Ops Level 5), which researches them from Advanced Data Samples, and those come from Exploration Points. It needs power, water and Science crew, so build it before you run out of Tier 2 options.
  • The waiting game. Half of this game is waiting for crew to arrive. I know what needs doing, but there aren't enough hands to do it. My tips: order the next crew member the moment one lands, so there's always one on the way. Fast-forward while a shuttle is inbound. Queue up cables, beacons and investigations so new arrivals have work waiting.
  • Keep an eye on the ship. The U.S.S. Resonant's crew quarters are at 18 of 25, the Trauma Ward is empty (0 of 10) and Officer Quarters is still locked. Storage is barely used at 128 of 1,050.
Current objective: Study the Plant, which means sending crew to investigate the Elder Plant.

Why I scrapped my first save

My first outpost got to around Day 50 and then hit a wall all at once: crew quarters were full, the Advanced Laboratory had no water and no samples, haulers couldn't keep up, and the ship's Mess Hall had a capacity of zero. Everything was waiting on something else.

So I started a new voyage on Easy, this time planning around those bottlenecks from the start.

Lesson: before you build a new building, check you have the quarters, crew, power and supplies to actually run it. An unstaffed building is just an expensive decoration.

What each Ops level unlocks

You level up the Operations Center by earning Outpost Proficiency: 30 for Level 3, 75 for Level 4, 200 for Level 5, 500 for Level 7 and 750 for Level 8. Some upgrades need materials as well: Level 7 needs 18 Polyalloy and 2 Duotronic Chips (see the Day 53 update). When it's ready, the top bar says READY TO UPGRADE. Click it, then Start Upgrade.

After an upgrade, any build tab with something new on it shows an orange corner flag. Open each one so you don't miss anything.

Level 4 costs

  • Crystal Collector: 6 Polyalloy + 1 Advanced Part
  • Crystals Extractor: 8 Polyalloy + 1 Advanced Part. Needs power and compute, and must sit on a Crystal Deposit.
  • Medical Facility: 6 Polyalloy + 1 Advanced Part
  • Planetary Habitat: 6 Polyalloy. Needs power, water and compute.
  • Crew Mess: 6 Polyalloy + 1 Advanced Part. Needs power and water.

Polyalloy: the thing everything needs

Look at that list again: almost every Level 4 building needs Polyalloy. Get it flowing early.

  • Alloy Refinery: costs 10 metal + 1 Advanced Part, needs power and compute, and is staffed by Engineering crew. It turns 4 metal into 1 Polyalloy. I staff mine on both day and night shifts.
  • Metal Recovery Unit: 14 Polyalloy + 1 Advanced Part, needs power and compute, and must sit on a Metal Deposit.

Getting Tech Data from the lab

The Basic Laboratory's Material Research uses one Collected Sample Data per run and produces Tech Data. If it says Lacking Resources, you've run out of samples.

Samples come from investigating Exploration Points, or from a Scientific Sample Collector.

What's the orange Tier 3 research point? It's the Advanced Tech Point, and nearly all of Tier 3 in the Tech Tree needs it. The Advanced Laboratory makes it from Advanced Data Samples, and those only come from Exploration Points. If you have none, the Advanced Lab just sits there saying "Lacking Resources". Switch it off and use those crew as haulers until you've found some Exploration Points.

The Requisitions Platform

  • Click the platform building itself to open it.
  • Hover each requested item to see where it comes from. Skip orders that need things you can't make yet.
  • If an order stalls, find out why: recipe switched off, no crew, or no haulers. If you can't fix it quickly, Cancel it and pick another.
  • After approving a departure there's roughly a 30-second cooldown. Wait, check again, then take the next order.

Crew: promotion, utility crew and quarters

  • Promoting: click the crew member → Promote → confirm. They need a full XP bar and good morale. Ensign to Lieutenant JG costs 2 prestige; Lieutenant JG to Lieutenant costs 3.
  • Utility crew are anyone not assigned to a building. They do the building and hauling. In Crew Overview → Tasks → Utility Crew, the arrow moves them between day and night shifts.
  • Crew Quarters cap: the Ship view shows how many crew you can hold. That's what stopped my first save. Expand it through the Tech Tree.
  • Resupply (first icon on the bottom-centre bar): when a crew member is energised down, order the next one straight away. What's on offer each time is random.
  • Need more utility crew? The + next to Utility Crew (Crew Overview → Tasks) only moves someone from the other shift. To free someone up, open a building, click the crew member's portrait under Crew Assignment, and pick the empty slot (the first icon in the list). They go straight to utility crew. Good places to take them from: buildings that are switched off, "Lacking Resources" or at Max Inventory.
Ship's Mess Hall empty? Go to the Ship, click Mess Hall, and check Food Proteins Capacity and Quality Meal Capacity. If they're 0, the ship never gets any food. Press + and food is sent up on its own.

Power, water and computer networks

Wait until buildings are finished, then connect them at their green connection points. Build one continuous spine rather than lots of separate bits. Water pipes and computer cables work the same way.

Storms and threats

  • Shelter: press J or the shelter icon at the bottom right. It's a toggle, so check whether it's already on before you press it.
  • Threats (Rhizome Probe, Hunter Roots): click the alert, then Respond to Threat.

Building and exploring tips

  • Investigating: click the actual plant or rock → Investigate → assign crew → Start. Hover the inputs to see what it needs.
  • Placing buildings: green means OK; yellow or red means blocked. V rotates.
  • Exploration beacons (Y): place them wide apart so they don't overlap.
  • Minimap: the magnifier and circle icons are exploration points. Click them to jump there.
  • Decorations: benches add comfort and the Starfleet Insignia adds purpose, but only where crew actually work or walk.
  • Food chain: Organics Collector → Biomass Synthesizer → Planetary Kitchen → Crew Mess / ship Mess Hall.
  • Planet to ship: Cargo Landing Pad → Manage Shipment moves goods between the two.

Stuck on the Ship screen? Click the Planet icon at the bottom left to get back to your outpost. The same icon also closes the Tech Tree and Crew Overview.
Accidentally in Photo Mode? The Starfleet logo at the bottom left is Photo Mode, not a menu. Press Esc to get your screen back.
Game running too fast? The speed buttons are at the top, just left of the day dial: pause, normal, fast and fastest. Worth checking before you leave the game running.
